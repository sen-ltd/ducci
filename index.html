Ducci numbers around a circle, minus their neighbours

Write numbers around a circle. A move replaces every number by its distance to the next one: (a₁, …, aₙ) ↦ (|a₁ − a₂|, |a₂ − a₃|, …, |aₙ − a₁|). Nothing grows, so every start ends in a cycle — and the cycle is always c times a pattern of 0s and 1s (Burmester, Forcade & Jacobs 1978). When n is a power of two the cycle is all zeros (Ciamberlini & Marengoni 1937). On 0s and 1s the move is x XOR rotate(x): cellular automaton rule 102 on a ring.
